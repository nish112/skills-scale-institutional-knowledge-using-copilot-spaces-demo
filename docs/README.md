# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

Welcome to the OctoAcme Project Management documentation. This is the entry point for understanding how OctoAcme plans, executes, and continuously improves its projects. Use this page to quickly navigate to the process documents that matter to your role.

## Project Management Lifecycle

OctoAcme runs projects through a structured, iterative lifecycle:

- **Initiation** — Validate the business need, define measurable outcomes, and align stakeholders before work begins.
- **Planning** — Build a prioritized backlog with clear acceptance criteria and a Definition of Done, and identify milestones, risks, and dependencies.
- **Execution and Tracking** — Deliver iteratively using the project board, collaborate through pull requests, rely on CI checks, and keep the team aligned with standups and demos.
- **Risk Management and Communication** — Maintain a risk register, monitor dependencies, share status regularly, and follow clear escalation paths.
- **Release and Deployment** — Verify acceptance criteria, pass CI and security checks, publish release notes, prepare rollback plans, test in staging, and verify production after deployment.
- **Retrospectives and Continuous Improvement** — Capture learnings, identify improvements, assign action items, and measure their impact.
